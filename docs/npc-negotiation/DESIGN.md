# NPC-led negotiation and follow-through

## Ownership

NpcInitiative remains the authoritative lifecycle for motive selection, approach, invitation, physical opportunity, completion and subsequent intentions. ConversationResolver resolves spoken actions and records semantic receipts. ConversationView presents the resolved words and choices. The focused `NpcNegotiation` helper maintains the ongoing exchange in the existing conversation checkpoint; it selects bounded continuations and calls the resolver for their actual effects.

SocialMemory owns heard claims, provenance, conditions, promises, secrecy and historical interactions. StrategicWorkPlanner and StrategicObjectivePlanner continue choosing useful work from owned knowledge. StrategicTaskSystem continues executing and reporting assignments. AllianceSystem and DealSystem retain their existing relationship/agreement boundaries. ScrambleStrategy and certified convergence retain individual voting authority. A spoken promise, proposed protection agreement or alternate target does not assign a human ballot. Pre-immunity proposal/refusal copy remains hypothetical, and deadline pressure requires an active post-immunity strategy model.

## Exchange checkpoint

The existing `checkpoint.npcNegotiation` holds initiator/listener IDs, intention/activity references, original and current subjects, semantic opening type, proposal history, current status, bounded response IDs, answered-question keys, attempted arguments, conditions and round count. This is exchange state; supporting facts remain in canonical memory. Save/load persists it through the existing production JSON contract.

Statuses distinguish an awaiting answer, spoken settlement, unresolved discussion and pending condition. Each proposal retains its own semantic receipt, subject, reason and status: pending, rejected, challenged, accepted, conditional or withdrawn. The initiating objective continues to exist independently of these proposals. A rejected pitch remains part of history when the next subject becomes active.

Duplicate action IDs/replayed receipts cannot advance the exchange or fire effects again. Explicit withdrawal can revise a previously settled exchange, with the canonical obligation tracking withdrawal. Unrelated promises and topic changes do not accept the current proposal.

## Counterproposal lifecycle

An explicit refusal records rejection. A flexible NPC may offer one legitimate alternate from its own strategic evaluation, excluding the participants and previous subjects and passing resolver validation. The alternate resolves as a real pitch, activates its own pending proposal, updates the subject and reopens relevant response choices. A listener's alternative pitch receives the ordinary independent NPC evaluation before the initiator considers it as a compromise. The exchange permits at most two counteroffer changes and five or six substantive rounds according to the existing character profile.

Questions about the alternate address that alternate. Conditional acceptance records the exact condition; it does not become guaranteed support. A bluff uses the existing cover-promise semantics and explicitly signals intentional fabrication to the human choosing it. The NPC receives the spoken commitment, without access to hidden human intentions.

The initiator can retain its original target even while exploring a compromise. A concession invalidates its objective evaluation cache so the next legitimate planning opportunity can assess the new information. It does not force either contestant to vote the compromise.

## Questions and persuasion

Why, evidence, source and numbers use resolver answers derived from the speaker's owned information. An uncorroborated personal strategic assessment is described as an assessment. The NPC's own recent pitch is not evidence proving itself. Source-protected information is withheld or attributed safely; an unprotected disclosure creates a real attributed claim and source chain.

Hesitation remains consideration. Where appropriate, an initiator can share an owned external claim it does not remember already disclosing to that listener, propose mutual protection through DealSystem, or make one urgent direct request. The existing character profile influences flexibility, relationship-based reassurance, privacy and pressure. Proposed human deals still require explicit acceptance. Novelty uses the initiator’s own attributed disclosure records; it never queries the listener’s private knowledge. The exchange remembers attempted arguments and repeated question keys, and stops rather than repeating the same pitch indefinitely. Generic categories, a topic change and ending remain available throughout.

Nondisclosure is a subjectless action. Memory records that the speaker declined to disclose plans; it creates no vote commitment and reveals no intended vote. Its transcript states what was actually said. Refusal, consideration, conditional support, firm spoken support, deceptive support and withdrawal preserve their existing semantic distinctions.

## NPC/NPC parity

Existing NPC meetings keep their physical reservation and canonical agenda execution. The listener's resolved adoption response supplies the first negotiation answer. No second adoption roll is taken for the same opening. A bounded continuation can ask for a firmer answer, interpret refusal, offer an alternate or preserve the listener's conditions. New speech receives ordinary resolver/strategy evaluation and independent listener responses.

The full live exchange need not persist after a completed NPC meeting: its compact proposal summary, conditions and follow-up subject remain in initiative state; full statements and receipts remain in canonical memory. Public continuations include physically present group members from the exact same player-conversation reservation and resolve their individual answers. The primary human negotiation remains unanswered even if another group member commits. Unrelated reservations and bystanders are excluded. Group participants retain independent answers. Task-agenda execution remains in the existing task adapter.

## Unfinished conditions and approach opportunity

An important owned conditional answer can schedule up to two prerequisite conversations, even before an early coalition is mature enough for a high-level objective. Each prerequisite names a valid required voter and actual voting subject, requires credible owned information and sufficient remaining time, and must be performed physically. Unknown support is never invented. Existing WorkPlanner task generation can also pursue the prerequisite. Returning to the original listener requires legitimately owned fulfillment and a relevant current plan.

Nearby contestants doing ordinary interruptible water, food or fire work may hear an approach. Travel, absence, exclusive conversations and protected work remain unavailable. An important initiator may wait briefly for a nearby listener's observably imminent completion. Waiting is bounded independently of travel/search retries; it does not permit remote activity tracking. Existing last sightings and actual arrival continue to control searches for moving listeners.

Fresh credible owned strategic information or a known accepted unfinished task can trigger reevaluation at a semantic boundary. A derived checkpoint stamp avoids rescanning the same evidence repeatedly. Protected conversations, travel and existing strategic activity are not interrupted. The same recency guard used by normal planning applies before routine work is abandoned. Persisted evidence signatures prevent repeated reprioritization after reload; the small runtime stamp is derived and reset safely on restore.

## Private relocation transaction

Before changing an invitation, the coordinator chooses an existing camp destination and checks real co-presence, route, remaining time and movement eligibility. CampActivitySystem can preflight releasing only that invitation's exact NPC waiting reservation. The invitation is cleared only when movement can commit. If the movement commit fails, the original NPC activity, invitation and intention state are restored. Failure provides a spoken explanation and keeps Talk now available. Successful relocation uses existing paired travel; neither participant teleports.

## Presentation and diagnostics

Current proposal responses appear above generic categories and always use the active subject. NPC follow-up speech and material non-human responses enter the transcript in resolved order, once. No private motive, confidence, deception probability or hidden vote is rendered. A failed private move refreshes its explanation without dismissing the invitation.

QA records unique intention/stage pairs separately from repeated attempt events. Human questions, consideration, conditions, spoken agreements, resistance, alternative proposals and later actions sharing a subject are observed separately. A shared subject establishes observable correlation, not proof that an NPC caused the later human action. Scripted human policies prove available influence paths; they do not predict real human persuasion.
