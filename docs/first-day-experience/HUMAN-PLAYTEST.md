# iPhone playtest — one season opening

Use a new season on your normal iPhone browser. No debug mode or special cast is needed. Keep short notes or phone screenshots. A quiet camp or an immunity win is a valid outcome; do not restart to manufacture drama.

- **Arrival:** choose your contestant and tribe setup. Can you identify yourself, your tribe and your role? Use Step into camp. Is your next action obvious?
- **Camp:** use Move around camp and the arrows. At the jungle, open the resource menu while people are nearby; try Back to camp and a work choice. In landscape, scroll to the lower resource choices. Can you tell who is nearby, working, arriving, leaving or talking together? Help with one task and talk to one person. Does it feel useful rather than administrative?
- **Attention:** spend some time near other people without initiating every exchange. If someone approaches, try Talk now, Give me a minute or Not right now. Does nothing start without your consent? Can you continue what you were doing?
- **Conversation:** ask a relevant follow-up, change topic, go Back and end the chat. Can you distinguish consideration, a promise and an intentional bluff? Does the NPC sound like a person and react to what you said?
- **Other games:** observe a nearby group. Do you see independent activity without being told private content? If a meeting moves away, does following cost real time?
- **Challenge:** continue through Tree Mail and immunity. Are the tribe names correct? Does the return feel connected to the result? If your tribe wins, continue until a legitimate loss; do not force one.
- **Scramble:** use Observe camp · 1 minute, then act. Reading should be safe; observation should advance the world. Ask two people where things stand. Can you distinguish their claims, uncertainty and your own promises using What I Know?
- **Tribal:** choose your own vote. Before voting, write down your expectation and uncertainty. Afterward, can you connect the result to what you heard and did, even if you were misled?
- **Recovery:** use Save Game from the menu, then reload during a conversation or pending approach. Is the same exchange restored without extra promises or sudden movement?
- **Phone ergonomics:** rotate once in camp and once in a long conversation. Check lower buttons, independent scrolling, safe areas and touch targets. Record any Safari-only problem.

At the end answer: Who felt distinctive? When did you lack a meaningful action? What surprised you for an understandable reason? Did you want to play the next episode? Name the single biggest obstacle to enjoyment.

This checklist supplies the human evidence automated Chromium testing cannot establish. Do not treat absence of an approach in one season as a bug by itself.
